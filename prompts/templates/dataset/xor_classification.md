XOR binary classification on tabular features. Features are drawn i.i.d. from a standard normal; the label depends on the *sign agreement* of two active coordinates, so no linear function of the inputs separates the classes at all — a model must compose the two coordinates multiplicatively. Any remaining coordinates are pure distractors. The label is the sign of the latent score against a calibrated threshold. The dataset card below states the active coordinates and the threshold. Every candidate trains on the same materialized train/test splits.
