Synthetic tabular binary classification. Features are drawn i.i.d. from a standard normal; a latent score `s(x)` over a few active coordinates is thresholded at a fixed cut-off chosen near the median of `s(x)`, so the two classes are close to balanced. The score is non-linear in the features, so no linear function of the inputs reproduces the boundary. The dataset card below states the exact rule family, active coordinates, weights, threshold, and the class balance it produces. Every candidate trains on the same materialized train/test splits.
